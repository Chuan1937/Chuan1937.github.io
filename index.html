About

Research through reproducible software

My work sits at the intersection of exploration geophysics, artificial intelligence, and scientific computing. I am particularly interested in turning rigorous numerical and signal-processing methods into clear, reusable research software.

This site is the central entry point for my research projects, publications, technical notes, and software documentation. Project documentation remains maintained in each project's existing repository and website.
